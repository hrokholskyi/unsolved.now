What this is

Product directories list solutions. This index lists the other side: problems that keep resurfacing in public discussions — specific enough to build against, sourced enough to trust. Every entry links the discussions it was curated from, states who has the problem, what they do about it today, and an editorial read on why it remains open. Nothing is fabricated: no invented vote counts, no fictional users, no made-up numbers.

What’s here now, honestly:

  • phase 1 · live — a curated catalog seeded from public discussions, one indexable page per problem, submissions via GitHub.
  • phase 2 · planned — structured community validation: frequency, severity, tools tried, willingness to pay, interview availability.
  • phase 3 · planned — verified demand profiles builders can act on, and problem → builder matching.
