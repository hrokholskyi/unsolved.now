What this is

A blackboard of the questions that are still open — the ones textbooks quietly skip past. Each entry states precisely what is unknown, how long it has resisted, what the best minds have managed so far, and what happens the day it falls. Every date, prize, and progress claim is cited to an authoritative source; nothing is invented or embellished.

  • now — a curated board across four fields, sources verified at curation time.
  • next — status watch: when a problem on this board falls, the board says so, fast.
  • later — the frontier: who is attacking each problem, and with what.
